Which Vans Go Electric?

EV fleet analysis — runs entirely in your browser

Every van is checked against every EV model for range, payload and 5-year saving.

Results include a data quality report, check figures, the shortlist, assumptions and CSV downloads.

Vendor exports are accepted as delivered: several trip files are combined and column names are mapped.

Range rule (Ops): a van qualifies only if its worst day fits within 60% of the EV WLTP range.
